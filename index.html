    Rules & options
    • Every person, the victim included, is on exactly one square.
    • No two people share a row or a column.
    • People can stand on open floor, or be on a chair, bed or carpet. Nobody can be on a square with a table, shelf, plant, TV, crate or sink.
    • A piece of furniture that spans several squares counts on each of them.
    • “Beside” means on a square that shares an edge.
    • The murderer is the only person alone in a room with the victim.
    • Row 1 is the north edge of the board, so “south” means further down the page.
